# 🧠 MindGuard AI

## AI-Integrated Mobile Application for Early Mental Health Risk Detection and Teleconsultation

> MindGuard AI is a proposed AI-integrated Android mobile application designed for preliminary mental-health risk screening and providing a structured pathway toward professional support and teleconsultation.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

MindGuard AI focuses on the early identification of potential mental-health risks related to **Depression, Anxiety, and Stress**.

The proposed application will use a structured questionnaire and a **Random Forest** machine-learning approach to provide a preliminary risk assessment.

The complete proposed flow is:

**Risk Detection → Recommendation → Professional Search → Appointment → Teleconsultation**

The teleconsultation pathway is proposed to support:

- 🎥 Video Consultation
- 🎙️ Audio Consultation
- 💬 Chat Consultation

> **Disclaimer:** MindGuard AI is intended for preliminary screening and awareness only. It is not a medical diagnosis system and does not replace a qualified mental-health professional.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

Mental-health concerns such as depression, anxiety, and stress can develop gradually and may remain unnoticed. Users may also face difficulties in recognizing potential concerns, performing an initial assessment, finding appropriate professionals, scheduling appointments, and accessing consultation conveniently.

MindGuard AI proposes an integrated platform that connects preliminary mental-health screening with recommendations and professional teleconsultation.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Develop an Android-based application for preliminary mental-health risk screening.
- Identify potential indicators related to depression, anxiety, and stress.
- Provide a structured mental-health questionnaire.
- Use a Random Forest model for preliminary risk assessment.
- Investigate suitable publicly available datasets from PhysioNet.
- Provide recommendations based on assessment results.
- Provide professional search functionality.
- Provide an appointment-booking pathway.
- Provide video, audio, and chat-based teleconsultation.
- Integrate screening and professional support into a single application workflow.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📝 Mental Health Questionnaire

A structured questionnaire will collect responses related to depression, anxiety, and stress.

### 🤖 AI-Based Risk Assessment

The proposed Random Forest model will process relevant inputs to generate a preliminary risk assessment.

### 📊 Risk Detection

The system will provide an indication of potential mental-health risk based on the assessment process.

### 💡 Recommendations

Users will receive appropriate guidance based on their preliminary assessment.

### 👨‍⚕️ Professional Search

Users will be able to search for relevant mental-health professionals.

### 📅 Appointment Booking

Users will be able to proceed toward appointment scheduling.

### 📞 Teleconsultation

The proposed system will support:

- Video consultation
- Audio consultation
- Chat consultation

### 📱 User Dashboard

A dashboard will organize relevant user activities and application information.

### 🕒 Assessment History

Previous assessment information can be maintained for reviewing the user's assessment journey.
